Privacy Policy

Rota for macOS & Rota for iOS · Last updated: 26 July 2026

The short version

Rota collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no identifiers. Everything Rota knows stays on your device.

What Rota accesses on your device

Rota reads what your music player (Apple Music, or Spotify in the Spotify-enabled build) is currently playing — title, artist, album, playback position and the album cover — solely to display it and to send your commands (play, pause, skip, seek) back to the player. On macOS this uses Apple Events with your explicit permission; on iOS it uses Apple's MediaPlayer framework with the Media & Apple Music permission.

The only two network requests

  • Lyrics — the current track's title, artist, album and duration are sent over HTTPS to LRCLIB, an open lyrics database, to fetch the words. No account, no keys, and the request contains nothing about you.
  • Missing covers — if a track exposes no artwork, the title/artist/album are sent over HTTPS to Apple's public iTunes Search API to find the cover image.

Responses are cached locally (~/Library/Caches/Rota on macOS). Neither request is linked to your identity, and nothing else ever leaves your device.

Data we store

Rota keeps small local files (last played track, cover, window position, preferences) on your device only, so the widget can restore itself after a reboot. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third parties

There are none: no SDKs, no analytics providers, no advertising networks.
